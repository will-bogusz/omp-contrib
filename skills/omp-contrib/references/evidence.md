# Evidence ledger

Every rule in this skill (`../SKILL.md` and its references), with its public source. `#n` is a
can1357/oh-my-pi PR or issue; a short SHA is a commit on its `main`. Upstream files are read at
`origin/main` of your checkout (`paths.checkout`); the corpus figures come from `gh` queries on
2026-09-28 (883 merged external PRs 08-08..09-28, 1,000 issues 08-30..09-20, 350 review threads
on 80 PRs). Counts of "a contributor's PRs" are from one contributor who ran this flow from
September to October 2026 (about 300 PRs merged in October). Where a rule rests on that
contributor's own runs and no public artifact shows it, the row gives the reason in one clause.

## Own-words sentence

- The requirement: `CONTRIBUTING.md` § *Pull request requirements*, added in `bb15939414`
  (2026-07-23). The template comment is in `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`.
- Agents may not generate it: `AGENTS.md` § *Pull requests* ("If it is missing, ask the
  contributor; NEVER generate a substitute"), added in `9048d71f49` (2026-09-06).
- #8577's final body has no own-words sentence and no placeholder: what happens when nobody asks.
